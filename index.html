🖤 TKDB Companion — now in open beta

Built for one reason: raising affinity and unlocking romantic dialogue should be something you look forward to every day. Roam the campus, run into the boys, pick how you respond, and watch a relationship actually grow line by line.

Commands

Command What it does Cooldown
/roam Wander Darkwick and randomly run into someone, somewhere. You get a scene, a line from them, and a choice of how to approach. 3 hours
/meet Skip the wandering — pick from 4 characters who catch your eye and go straight to them. 3 hours
(separate timer from /roam)
/affinity <name> [up to 5 names] Check your relationship level with one or more characters (e.g. /affinity rui leo towa). None
/house See which of the 8 houses you're closest to, based on your total bonds. None
How a bond grows: after each encounter you choose a reply — Kind, Playful, Bold, or Neutral. Each character secretly favors a different style. Match what they like and the bond climbs faster; a mismatched or neutral reply barely moves it — so you have to actually read them. Levels rise Stranger → Acquaintance → Friend → Close Friend → Confidant → Devoted → Soulbound.

🤯 Wow facts

  • 26 characters, each written across 6 distinct relationship tiers — the same person greets you completely differently as a Stranger vs. a Confidant vs. Soulbound. The flirtation tier unlocks at Close Friend; the full romantic tier only opens at Devoted and Soulbound.
  • Nearly 3,500 hand-written lines in total: 822 scene-narration lines, 780 spoken in-character greetings, 624 "make your move" button phrasings, and 1,248 response-button phrasings — roughly 130 fresh lines per relationship tier.
  • A single /roam with one character at one tier can play out 100+ different ways (narration × spoken greeting × approach button), and that's before the reply step, which draws its 4 buttons from 81 possible layouts. Across all 26 characters and all 6 tiers that's over 1.2 million distinct encounter states.
  • 135 hand-painted backdrops, many with separate day and dusk lighting, composited live under the character art. Count the character-on-location renders alone and you're past 6,000 unique scene images; fold in dialogue and the visually-distinct combinations run into the hundreds of millions.
  • Characters dress down as they trust you. Everyone starts in uniform. Casual outfits start slipping in at Acquaintance (~25%) and by Soulbound they're in casual wear 95% of the time. 22 of the 26 have a full second wardrobe.
  • The world runs on your clock. Some locations only render in the afternoon/evening, and at least one character (Towa) will only appear after noon — with his own separate set of morning-vs-night lines.
  • It's a genuine slow burn. Reaching Soulbound with someone takes hundreds of well-read responses. With a couple of check-ins a day, every character is a months-long arc. That's the point.
