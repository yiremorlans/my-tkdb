🖤 My Tkdb, now in open beta

Built for one reason: raising affinity and unlocking romantic dialogue should be something you look forward to every day. Roam the campus, run into the boys, pick how you respond, and watch a relationship actually grow line by line.

A fan-made project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by ZigZaGame Inc.

✨ What's new

  • Bond scenes in your DMs. Every time you reach a new relationship level, that character messages you privately with a short scene written just for that moment, ending on a choice of your own. There's no timer: leave mid-scene and pick it back up whenever you like. Each one leaves you a keepsake, and you can replay any finished scene from /bonds character:<name>.
  • Call-out encounters. Every so often a dark silhouette appears in your server's encounter channel. Be the first to name them with /call and the post reveals who it was, with a line written for your relationship with them. A win gives you a bonus point on your next /roam or /meet with that character and adds to your Moments together in /affinity.
  • Inspector missions. Six times a day, at shifting times, a mission request goes up on the board. First to click Accept takes it, and only you find out what it is:
    • Riddle: an anomaly report describes a student without naming them. Work out who it is with /riddle.
    • Errand: collect signatures from students of one house (they turn up more often on your /roam and /meet while it's open), then file the report with /docs. If you're lucky, the Chancellor may let you /request a different house.
    • Co-op: call for backup with /mission assist:true. The first inspector to join clears it with you.
    Missions earn house logs, which set your Inspector rank in /house, plus a banked cooldown reset. The next time /roam or /meet tells you to wait, you get a button to spend it and jump straight in.
  • Countdown channels. Servers can set a channel that counts down to the next Tokyo Debunker Wishing Well or to their own events. Anyone can check upcoming Wishing Well dates in their own timezone with /wishingwell.

Commands

Command What it does Cooldown
/roam Wander Darkwick and randomly run into someone, somewhere. You get a scene, a line from them, and a choice of how to approach. 3 hours
/meet Skip the wandering: pick from 4 characters who catch your eye and go straight to them. 3 hours
(separate timer from /roam)
/affinity <name> [up to 5 names] Check your relationship level and Moments together with one or more characters (e.g. /affinity rui leo towa). None
/bonds Everyone you've bonded with, ranked closest first. Add character: to look back on your scenes and keepsakes with one person and replay them, or dms:off to stop bond scene DMs. None
/house Your Inspector dossier: rank, house logs, the mission you're holding, and the house your bonds lean toward. None
/call <name> Name the silhouette in the encounter channel. First correct answer wins. 10 seconds after a wrong name
/mission Read the briefing for the mission you're holding, or see when the next request lands. You can accept up to 2 missions a day. None
/riddle <answer> Riddle missions: name the student behind the anomaly report. 60 seconds after a wrong answer
/docs Errand missions: check your signatures and file the report. None
/wishingwell See upcoming Wishing Well dates in your own timezone. None
Server admins: /encounters and /missions choose where call-outs and mission requests post, and /countdown and /event set up countdown channels. All of them need the Manage Server permission.
How a bond grows: after each encounter you choose a reply: Kind, Playful, Bold, or Neutral. Each character secretly favors a different style. Match what they like and the bond climbs faster; a mismatched or neutral reply barely moves it, so you have to actually read them. Levels rise Stranger → Acquaintance → Friend → Close Friend → Confidant → Devoted → Soulbound.

🤯 Wow facts

  • 26 characters, each written across 6 distinct relationship tiers. The same person greets you completely differently as a Stranger vs. a Confidant vs. Soulbound. The flirtation tier unlocks at Close Friend; the full romantic tier only opens at Devoted and Soulbound.
  • 156 hand-written bond scenes, one for every character at every level, and no two share a line.
  • Characters dress down as they trust you. Everyone starts in uniform, and the closer you get, the more often they show up in casual wear.
  • The world runs on the clock. Some locations only appear in the evening, and some characters say different things depending on the time of day.
  • It's a genuine slow burn. Reaching Soulbound with someone takes hundreds of well-read responses. With a couple of check-ins a day, every character is a months-long arc. That's the point.
