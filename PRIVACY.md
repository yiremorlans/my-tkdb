# Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 27, 2026

My Tkdb ("the Application") is a private, personal-use Discord application hosted
on GitHub. This policy explains what the Application keeps while it runs in the
private Discord server(s) where it operates.

My Tkdb is an unofficial, fan-made project. It is **not affiliated with, endorsed
by, sponsored by, or approved by ZigZaGame Inc.** Tokyo Debunker and all related
characters, names, artwork, and other materials are the property of ZigZaGame
Inc. and are used here for non-commercial fan purposes only.

In short: the Application keeps **game data**, such as your progress with each
character, what you have unlocked, and when you last played. It does not keep
personal information about you.

## 1. What the Application keeps

To remember your game between sessions, the Application stores data keyed to
your **Discord user ID**, a number Discord assigns to your account:

- **Relationship progress:** affinity with each character, how many times you
  have met, when you last met, and the type of your last response.
- **Activity counts:** how many commands you have used (lifetime and this
  month), which days you were active, and when each command was used. The
  game uses these for cooldowns and for character reactions to your play
  habits, for example noticing you usually play late or have been away a while.
- **Unlocks and records:** bond scenes you have received and your choice in
  each, keepsakes, encounter and mission results, house points, and store
  credit.
- **Optional birthday:** if you choose to enter one, the **month and day only**
  (never a year), used to send a birthday message in the game. You can clear
  it at any time with `/birthday clear`.
- **Message bookkeeping:** the IDs of the Discord channels and messages the
  Application posts (including the DM channel used for bond messages), so it
  can update or continue them.

## 2. What the Application does not keep

- Your username, display name, avatar, email address, or any other profile
  details.
- The content of your messages. The Application only reads the commands and
  buttons you use with it.
- IP addresses, device information, or location.

## 3. How the data is used

Only to run the game: tracking progress, enforcing cooldowns, and personalizing
character dialogue and messages. Any aggregate statistics (for example, which
characters are most popular) are computed through views that do not expose
Discord user IDs.

## 4. Retention

Usage logs and resolved encounter records are deleted automatically after
about 90 days. Progress, unlocks and records you can see in the game (affinity,
keepsakes, bond scenes, mission history) are kept so your game is not lost.

## 5. Sharing

The data is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties. The Application operates through the official Discord API and is subject to
Discord's Developer Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. It uses no third-party
tracking, analytics, or advertising tools.

## 6. Your choices

- Turn off bond and occasion messages at any time with `/bonds dms:off`.
- Remove your birthday with `/birthday clear`.
- To have all of your game data deleted, contact the maintainer (see §8).

## 7. Changes

If the Application starts keeping a new kind of data, this document will be
updated before that change takes effect.

## 8. Contact

For questions, or to request deletion of your data, open an issue or contact
the maintainer on the My Tkdb GitHub repository page.
