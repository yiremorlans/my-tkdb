# Taiga

## Overview
Taiga Hoshibami (星喰大我 Hoshibami Taiga) is a third year student at Darkwick Academy and the captain of the Sinostra House, who owns of the extravagant casino housed there.

Owner of the extravagant Sinostra casino. Though an uncannily skilled gambler, his impulsive behavior money squandering put him in constant conflict with others, including his business partner Romeo.
"Quit squirming around. You that excited to be my next meal?"

## Appearance
Taiga is a man of average height and pale complexion with angular, hooded bright yellow-green eyes and sharp jagged rows of teeth. His hair is shabby and about chin length in the front and shoulder length in the back. It's a crimson red with a blond-ish undergrowth.

He's well built and typically dresses lazily wearing black slacks, an oversized wrinkled white button down with the top buttons undone, and red suspenders, but occasionally dresses nice when he needed.

He wears a multitude (two or three) gold chain necklaces and a few rings on each hand, along with a round big-sized gold bracelet on his right wrist, his nails are painted black, and he has a small gold earring on his left ear.

## Personality
Taiga is a reckless and impulsive person, willing to use violence and cause trouble until he gets what he wants. His behavior can be destructive and spontaneous during missions. He is very trigger-happy and willingly points loaded firearms at many people. This includes his "mooks", a group of general students who take orders from him,and his vice-captain, Romeo.

On the other hand, Taiga seems to be forgetful frequently, asking people who they are despite meeting them just yesterday. Though, this never apply to people whom he gave nicknames to (Romeo, Haru, Rui).

Although, Taiga always gets bored easily and tend to keep silent in most conversations, he's actually more of a genius. In the two missions "The Carnivorous Casino" and "The Auction Of No Return", it's likely that he has already known how to deal with the anomalies, which could be referred from his riddles.

He could also be found eating anomalies, for example, the like dove in Episode 4: The Carnivorous Casino and the anomalous mask in Episode 9: Spooks' Paradise. He also tries to eat Peekaboo but never succeeds in doing so.

In a flashback during Episode 22: Who Killed Robin? Act II, Elias says "You wouldn't know it to look at him, but Taiga's a sensitive soul. Maybe he's just waiting for someone who truly believes in him."

### Artifact
- Tommy gun - takes anything broken into small enough pieces and fired from it as ammo.
- Damage type seems to vary based on what was placed into it. Shards of glass created a swirl of glass shards, whereas bronze from a statue shot heavier rounds.
- Doesn't seem to require ammo to fire.
- Doesn't seem to work on everything as Taiga says his bullets don't work on anything lately. Possibly limited to hostile entities? (He was able to shoot the Oblivion Dealer, but not able to shoot the Kyklos--which ran away--or the Barong--which was non-threatening.)

### Other
- Carries a knife and a pistol.
- Appears to occasionally be aware of things that he has no way of knowing about, sometimes even seeming to know the future. Haru asks hin when several characters are returning, which suggests Haru is aware of this and occasionally asks him to predict outcomes.
- Taiga asks Haru for a coin, saying he'll allow Haru to choose his desired outcome based on heads or tails. However it's uncertain if this means he can actually alter outcomes himself. His losing dialogue suggests that he attempts to, but doesn't always succeed.
- Eats anomalies, though not exclusively, and gets hungry whenever he goes on a mission. His hunger for them results in varied behavior from minor irritation to nearly losing control of himself to the hunger. He can eat any physical anomaly thus far, being shown or referenced to eat anomalous animals, plants, objects, and "enzymes", and he finds anomalous plants and enzymes converted into beverages suitable for human consumption to taste unpleasant. He eats normal food and drinks normal alcohol without a problem. It's difficult to tell how he feels about and after eating anomalies, as it varies, although he enthusiastically expresses his desire to eat the Kyklos. It's hard to tell how new this behavior is as Romeo seems disgusted but not surprised by it and Taiga attempts to warns Romeo when he wants to eat the Barong mask(not in speech bubbles but able to be discerned in his audio.)
- His hunger may be able to be triggered or increased by smelling or seeing anomalous blood(such as the PC's.)
- His memory is very poor, although he can remember things and people with prompting.
- Noted that, as he's not Haru, he can't jump up a building.
