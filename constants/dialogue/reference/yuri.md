# Yuri

## Overview
The domineering captain of Mortkranken, which doubles as a medical facility and houses students who specialize in anomalous medicine. Sly and calculating, he will take any opportunity to turn anomalies and other ghouls into lab rats for his experiments.
“
Allow me to grant you the privilege of becoming my test subject.
”

## Appearance
Yuri has short, teal colored hair, teal eyes, pale white skin, a double lobe piercing on his right ear and a single lobe piercing on his left.

He is usually seen wearing his school uniform, which consists of a white button up shirt beneath a black buttoned up blazer, black pants, a black and teal necktie and white gloves.

He is also seen wearing his lab uniform which consists of a grey button up shirt, black pants, a black necktie with a teal stripe down the center, black gloves, a black leather chest harness, a black choker and an unbuttoned white lab coat.

## Personality
Yuri is an arrogant, snobby, and ambitious man with a lot of confidence in his abilities, calling himself the "genius and visionary Dr. Yuri Isami".

He is hardworking and determined, as he has a goal of being the pioneer of anomalous medical science.

His desire to be acknowledged by the world is his main motivator in making progress in his research, so he takes his duties as a physician seriously and puts his all into everything he does.

Yuri is diligent in his studies and expresses pride in his accomplishments, having a habit of showing off while taking pleasure in being recognized and praised. He is also fiercely competitive and tends to be quite aggressive with those he considers his rivals. He often flaunts his knowledge while looking down on others he deems less intelligent than himself.

In contrast to his stoic assistant, Jiro, Yuri appears to be a very expressive and easily flustered person. He is short-tempered and has difficulty controlling his emotions.

He has some quirks, like humming classical music and having a poor sense of direction. As apparent from his Affinity 23 and 24 dialogues, Yuri struggles with the idea of losing patients.

In Episode 14: The Infectious Death Wish when two of Frostheim students humiliated Yuri during a medical check-up, Yuri raises his voice at them, but shortly after is speechless. When the two students leave Yuri starts crying, revealing that he might be a much more sensitive and insecure person than he appears.
