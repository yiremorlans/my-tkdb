# Mio

## Overview
Mio Susuhara is a second year student and the vice captain of the Dionysia House at Darkwick Academy who was first mentioned in "Episode 12: The Cry Of A Cat" and made his debut as a cameo in "Episode 25: The Fiends' Lament".

The vice-captain of Dionysia and a master craftsman, Mio is the leader of Darkwick's clockwork artisans. From building stage sets and mission-ready tools to handling repairs across the houses, there's almost nothing he can't do—and requests for his handiwork are unending.
"We're alike? …That's a little offensive to her, don't you think?"
Wide intellect - Mio is known for his knowledge on machinery and how to tinker with them.

## Stigma & Artifact

Contains story spoilers: plot reveals and sensitive notes here are spark tier and up only, never new/known/warm. Lines tagged [spark+] are the flagged ones. "?" and question marks are the compiler's: unconfirmed or not yet revealed.

### Stigma
- Incantation - Samrab(Marbas)
- Effect - Allows him to see the construction of nonliving things
- He can see the floorplan for buildings, but not the location of people or objects within that aren't part of the building itself

### Artifact
- Clockwork Anomaly - Able to change its shape and use the eyes on it as a camera, among other uses?
- May be able to change its size too?

### Other
- Able to make pretty much anything with clockwork, from a pair of glasses that 3D prints accurate replicas to a machine that analyzes things recreated by the Observer in Sector 1 in order to alter them and fix the flavors that it was unable to replicate.
- Leads the clockwork artisans of Dionysia, essentially the head stagehand of the circus.
- Maintains structures and systems around Darkwick, including automatic doors, security systems, air conditioning, and more. Everything is significantly worse off in his absence, and the worker cats are overworked. Without him to lead the artisans, none of them seem to be tasked with maintenance.
- Is asked to do a great deal of work by Darkwick itself. Doesn't sleep much, but seems to be content this way.
- Made and maintains the secret passage to Romeo's secret office/auction room beneath Sinostra, and the machines in the casino.
- Made and maintains Haru and Romeo's prosthetic limbs
- "Has no worldly desires" and is happy to work for free. Enjoys making things just for the challenge and if they seem interesting.
