# Ren

## Overview
Ren Shiranami (白波 蓮 Shiranami Ren) is a first year student at Darkwick Academy and a member of the Jabberwock House. He was assigned to the endlessly busy Jabberwock House despite his total lack of motivation. He also works part-time at the school's Mystery Diner.

Assigned to the endlessly busy Jabberwock House despite his total lack of motivation, Ren's grievances are unending and his phone is his only sanctuary.
"That's harassment. Is everyone in this place from the past?"

## Appearance
Ren has dark greenish brown hair and light blue eyes. He has black stud earrings — 2 on the left and 1 on the right — as well as a digital watch on his left arm.

He wears the Jabberwock uniform.

In his Darkwick uniform, he wears a black and gold blazer over a grey buttoned shirt. Under those layers are a white collared shirt and the orange-black patterned tie for Jabberwock. There are gold chains adorned on the right shoulder of his blazer. His House is additionally symbolized by an orange gem lapel pin resting on his chest, and the inside of his blazer features orange. He has black pants.

## Personality
Looking at the expressions he showed when he was normally and accidentally pulled into a mission, he was quite scared and often avoided any trouble.

Always carrying his phone with him everywhere, Ren is not cowardly but in some cases he just wants to get away from that place as quickly as possible. Additionally, he uses reasons like “harassment” and “forced labor” to argue against what he is uncomfortable with. He constantly complains with the work he’s forced to do everyday and is desperate to avoid as much work as possible.

Ren hates anything water-related. And despite liking horror movies, he is scared of actual horror in front of him.

Although on the surface he seems rude and uncaring, Ren cares and thinks a lot. When necessary, he chooses to do the right thing, like when he saved Calamari from death. He also tends to hide his feelings.

Ren addresses the MC as "senpai" (lowercase when texting, capitalised when spoken).

House style note: beyond "senpai" itself, Ren's texting casing is irregular, not uniformly lowercase — sentence starts are a mixed bag cap/lowercase, but "I"-forms and proper nouns stay capitalized. Spoken lines use normal sentence capitalization.

Author note: Ren enjoys eating with company (the MC and/or Ritsu).
