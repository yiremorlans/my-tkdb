# Kaito

## Overview
Kaito Fuji (歩二 魁斗 Fuji Kaito) is a second year student at Darkwick Academy and a member of the Frostheim House.

A trend-chasing social media enthusiast, Kaito works hard to fit in with the Frostheim elites despite his modest upbringing, but his efforts are overshadowed by his tendency to be a gutless coward.
"How would you know what it's like to be at the bottom of the food chain?"

## Appearance
Kaito has dark hair dyed blond in an undercut, as well as turquoise eyes, and freckles. He wears a ring on his left thumb, as well as mismatched hoop earrings — silver on the left and gold on the right — as well as his most prized possession, a silver necklace given to him by his grandmother.

He wears the Frostheim uniform with the top button unbuttoned and tie slightly loose.

## Personality
Kaito is energetic and outgoing but hesitant to be in the spotlight, though this is typically overcome when a girl is present.

He is empathetic and cowardly, and was shown to be insecure as he constantly compares himself to Lucas and apologizes to MC frequently.

He is social online and tends to chase trends, though he struggles to form relationships with people outside of it.

Author note: Kaito is a physical touch person. With Towa, apart from hanging out and practicing dancing together, they hug and lean on each other a lot.

## Background
Kaito was born and raised in Tokyo by his grandma. At one point, his grandmother gifted him the pendant he currently wears.

In Episode 9, Romeo digs up a grave with an expensive but nameless headstone, and finds a bracelet with the same insignia on Kaito's pendant, remarking that he must be right about the pendant being special. In Episode 13, it is revealed that his pendant once belonged to the globally renowned family of the House of Ardici.

In Episode 20, it is revealed that Kaito's grandmother was the one who adopted him, and refuses to tell him what happened to his parents.

At some point, his grandmother grew ill and was revealed to have developed cancer. After deciding to go hang out with his classmates rather than check up on her in the hospital, Kaito grew guilty and decided to run straight back home, only to be greeted by a pot of curry with his grandmother's note on it.

### Stigma
- Incantation - Opsi(Ipos)
- [Ipos] is a Prince and powerful Earl of Hell (a Duke to some authors), having 36 legions of demons under his command. He knows and can reveal all things past, present, and future. He can make men witty and valiant. He is commonly depicted with the body of an angel with the head of a lion, the tail of a hare, and the feet of a goose, less frequently in the same shape but with the body of a lion, and rarely as a vulture.
- Effect - "can see auras sometimes"
- Does not need to be activated with his incantation, but appears to activate at random. Is able to activate it willingly when enhanced by MC.
- Auras have different colors for different people, allowing him to discern if an entity is the person they appear to be or not.
- Hyde didn't sound certain about what it does, presumably due to the lack of frequency and stability/control Kaito has over it.
- Kaito has notably enhanced vision, able to see things that are further away and surprisingly makes him skilled at reading lips.
- When Lucas's stigma is enhanced, Kaito is able to see his aura "go crazy". The same happens when Lucas is wounded and his swords react to his blood.
- Kaito is able to see Zenji's aura behind/with Saburo, but simply thinks Saburo's aura is strange.

- From the B's-LOG magazine October 2024 issue:
  - Q: What is good about your house?
  - A: Hm... my house, on the surface, is a symbol of authority! Or that's what's said. Honestly, commoners like me feel out of place... they're all always trying to get the upper hand, I can't stand it.
  - Q:  Do you have any concerns with missions?
  - A: Nonono, firstly I'm concerned that the mission itself even exists!? I just want to live a peaceful and fun school life!!
  - Q: Where would you like to go if you have your R&R permit approved?
  - A: Um... wait a sec. I think it was featured in Men's An'a*, but... ah, yeah yeah! The number 1 for classic dating spots ranked... the Tokyo Skytree!!! (*Possibly a parody of An An magazine)
  - Q: What do you think about the ghoul(s) in your house?
  - A: Hm... the captain and vice captain honestly feel like they live above the clouds... Rumor has it the two of them are colluding with Darkwick, I heard they're doing something nasty. Eh? Luca? ...I don't know that bastard with an unrivaled face.
- He is very active on WickHive and always knows of recent rumors and trends.
- Despite being a bow user, his aim is quite poor.
- Despite having enhanced strength as one of the perks of being a ghoul, according to Professor Hyde in the prologue, he believes Kaito isn't even strong enough to kill a cockroach.
- In Episode 1, he is revealed to wear limited-edition Nike shoes.
- Lucas is mentioned to have cut Kaito's hair in the Frostheim chapter.
- In Episode 2, it is revealed that he takes remedial classes for one of his failed subjects.
- Towa's nickname for Kaito is "Coriander" which is a symbol of vitality and health. Its seeds were once used in ancient China to create love potions.
- His character is heavily inspired by Zenitsu Agatsuma from the animanga series, Demon Slayer.
- The "Shonen Champ" magazine that Kaito mentions in Kaito & Lucas Chapter 1 Character Story is likely a reference to Weekly Shōnen Jump, a popular Japanese shōnen manga magazine.
- He appears to be right-handed, judging by his Campus writing animation.
- He reveals in Episode 25 that he once begged his grandmother to get him an acoustic guitar but he got bored of it after three days.
- Kaito uses the おれ [Ore] pronoun for himself and the お前 [Omae] pronoun for others.
  - Kaito occasionally uses the きみ [Kimi] pronoun for others instead of おまえ [Omae], as seen in Chapter 13 of the Prologue.
  - Kaito typically uses the おれ [Ore] instead of the kanji 俺 [Ore] for himself, the おまえ [Omae] instead of the kanji お前 [Omae] for others, and the きみ [Kimi] instead of the kanji 君 [Kimi] for others.
