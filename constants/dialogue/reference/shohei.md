# Shohei

## Overview
Shohei "Sho" Haizono (灰園翔平 Haizono Shohei) is a first year student at Darkwick Academy and a member of the Vagastrom House. He is also the younger brother of Professor Hyde.

A multitalented delinquent with diverse interest. His cocky attitude tends to rub people the wrong way. A capable cook, he is particular about his dishes.
"Pass. I'm not going on some kindergarten field trip with you."

## Appearance
Shohei has short white hair parted from his side, with layered bangs reaching his cheeks, blue eyes, and his notable dark blue bandana with grey detailing covering his forehead.

He also has a lobe piercing with a small silver hoop earring, and double helix piercings on the same ear with similar small bar earrings.

## Personality
Sho initially comes off as a typical delinquent. His way of speaking tends to come off a bit aggressive or apathetic.

A big deal of this is seen with Leo, though, despite his words, he does what he is asked. Sho is hardworking and responsible when it comes to his craft. He works hard to keep his food truck business going and takes pride in his work. He dislikes when people leave their food cold and despises it even more when people waste perfectly good food.

While he appears to tolerate Leo's antagonistic actions, he draws a line when Leo starts to mock his friends. This is shown when Subaru brings his motorbike to Kamata Prison when he heard they needed help. Leo starts to mock Subaru for not feeding Bonnie properly, and Sho reprimands him, telling him to knock it off.

Sho also develops a liking to tease his friends, particularly the MC.

Sho addresses the MC as "Senpai" — a running joke about her being a year ahead of him that stops being a joke. Lowercase when texting, capitalised when spoken.
