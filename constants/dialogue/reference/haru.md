# Haru

## Overview
Haru Sagara (艸楽 陽 Sagara Haru) is a third year student at Darkwick Academy and the captain of the Jabberwock House.

Haru spends day and night running around after the creatures in his care. Though laid back and relentlessly cheerful, he drags those around him into trouble with his antics.
"You'll be right, you got this! I believe in you!"

## Appearance
Haru has short red hair that fades into a dark gradient, with pale skin and an average build. He is the second shortest of the ghouls, after Kaito.

His Darkwick Academy uniform consists of an orange vest, a black and orange necktie, black gloves, black pants, a pin chain on his collar and ear piercings on both of his ears. The right ear is gauged with an earring through in the middle, while the left ear has an earring going through two holes in the earlobe.

He often has a teal sling bag on him used as a carrier for Peekaboo. Haru's right arm is a prosthetic with functional joints, which he keeps covered with his black gloves. The prosthetic was built for him by Mio Susuhara, Dionysia's vice-captain and resident craftsman, who also made Romeo's prosthetic limb. How Haru lost the arm is not established.

When he first transferred to Jabberwock, he had slightly longer hair on the back; it is also revealed in "Episode 22: Who Killed Robin? Act II", that when he fully opens his eyes, he has silver irises.

## Personality
Haru is a character who is known for his never-ending work as the captain of the Jabberwock House — Being cheerful and funny are also important factors along with his determination and hard work.

Haru is a very hard worker, spending most of his time dealing with anomalies and taking care of their needs, and is also quite good at withstanding the intense workload that comes with Jabberwock.

Moreover, Haru shows his determination and stubbornness to "put things in place" with the other house members. For example, he goes to great lengths to break Ren's motivation — such as putting a tracking device on him and forcing him out of his room.

Furthermore, he is constantly planning marketing strategies to boost the popularity of Jabberwock and its tour, by any means possible. These include unwittingly instigating/enabling flirtatious comments by the general Frostheim girls by offering a free boat ride with him if they get enough stamps, passing out flyers to anyone and everyone, even to MC, admitting he was so busy talking, he didn’t recognize her. He also will push the tour in any circumstance, even with inappropriate timing. An example would be Haru coming to Ren’s job and introducing himself to the MC in exchange for giving her “mates’ rates” to encourage her to come on the tour.

His encouraging and motivating words can prove that he is quite an optimistic person. Haru makes jokes and has a personality befitting a leader, proving himself fit to be the Jabberwock captain.

## Background
According to Ren, Haru transferred to Jabberwock from another house.[1] Janitor Elias later reveals that Haru used to be a member of Dionysia, where he was an acrobat.
