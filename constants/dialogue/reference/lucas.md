# Lucas

## Overview
Lucas Errant (ルーカス•エラント Rūkasu Eranto) is a second year student at Darkwick Academy and a member of the Frostheim House.

A transfer student from Darkwick's sister school in the UK. Lucas is friendly, honest, and chivalrous, but any mention of demons lights a cold fire in his heart. He is searching for his twin brother.
"I came to this school to learn how to subjugate demons."

## Appearance
Lucas Errant has tan skin, purple eyes, and ombre brown-to-purple hair. He also has three silver ear piercings; A set of helix piercings on both ears and the lobe piercing on his right ear.

## Personality
Lucas is known for his friendly and approachable nature, which makes others feel comfortable trusting him. He highly values honesty and honor, and conducts himself as a true gentleman. He is quick to step in to defend other around bullies.

However, his tendency to trust others easily and act impulsively sometimes leads to unforeseen consequences. Additionally, he struggles with keeping secrets to himself.
After receiving education at the prestigious British Branch, Emrys Academy, Lucas made the decision to transfer to Darkwick with the goal of delving deeper into the study of demons as part of his relentless pursuit to locate his missing twin brother. In Season 2 Episode 7, his brother's name is revealed to be named Nox.

His family is mentioned to be wealthy, just like most of the Frostheim students.
Wide intellect — Lucas was able to outsmart Romeo, who was asking for 2,000£ of interest from Kaito's debt, to let him read Kaito's papers again, making Romeo back off.

Author note: Lucas is a bit naive.

### Other
- Claims his brother was taken away by the demon he made a pact with. His goal is to subjugate a demon to make it tell him where his brother is.
- Carries around a knife.
- Believes one shouldn't do things on an empty stomach, which is part of why he carries the biscuits his family sends him in care packages with him. He claimed that his brother Nox loved them.
- Hates anomalies and tends to become less rational when it comes to them. Despite repeatedly being taught to capture, not kill, anomalies, even in practice/anomalous combat, Lucas will kill them and does not understand why this is the incorrect solution. He claims that Emrys Academy is significantly harsher when it comes to anomalies than Darkwick, which may contribute to his aggression, although it likely relates to his brother's absence. Becomes tense and angry when reminded that he, too, is an anomaly, and is angered by the idea that his stigma may be attached to an anomaly as well.
- Is prone to running ahead and thoughtlessly doing what he believes is right without considering those who have to catch up to him.
- His strict upbringing causes him to to seemingly not think for himself rather than only thinking for the way he was taught. He seems to think for himself more over time, even to the point of expressing concern over [humanlike, childlike] anomalies.
- He trains daily, as much as possible, whether it's exercise or actual training against fake anomaly targets, but also occasionally goes out late at night in search of demons.

## Canon guardrails
- the acquaintance and friend bond scenes are off-voice and need a rewrite with a new scenario. His `new`/`known` dialogue in lucas.js is the current voice reference. In that dialogue he is socially confident and warm, not hesitant. He smiles first, holds doors without a word, steps between a first-year and older bullies "calm as if it's nothing", takes your bag ("Allow me"), brings an extra biscuit, asks whether you've eaten and expects the honest answer, turns up ten minutes early, drills alone in the training yard, worries about Kaito, rehearses asking Jin for an assignment, and stays late over demon texts.
  What the old scenes got wrong: (1) Acquaintance has him fumbling ("I'd likely forget half of what I meant to say") and asking MC how to repay a kindness, with a hidden four-page ledger. That's passive, and debt/ledger framing is Romeo's and Ritsu's transactional motif. (2) Friend has people treating him "like a thing that might go off", which contradicts canon: people find him approachable and trust him easily. (3) "The first joke since I got here" makes him humorless. He's friendly and at ease, especially with Kaito and MC. (4) "Not always as well mannered as I look" reads as menace. The "out of sorts, like I'm not myself" thread is canon (LVL 24), but it belongs to later tiers, and he frames it as something to train through, not a threat.
  Canon material for new scenarios: stepping in to defend someone; early training invites ("An early training session will warm you up"); being ten minutes early; biscuits on hand; Kaito's ramen, pajama parties, and the Kaito + MC trio; Emrys vs. Darkwick discrepancies; UK folklore (the Sandman); fetching mission documents from Tohma; asking Jin for assignments; morning vs. night (his twin Nox preferred night); he struggles to keep secrets, so a friend-tier beat can be him telling MC something he'd meant to keep; "If you're ever in trouble, you can always come to me."
