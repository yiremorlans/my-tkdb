# Lyca

## Overview
Lyca Colt (ライカ・コルト Raika Koruto) is a first year student at Darkwick Academy and a member of the Obscuary House.

He is a werewolf ghoul previously imprisoned on campus, but thanks to MC and the ghouls of Hotarubi, he was allowed to become a student at the academy.

He is working hard to adapt to human society, but his lack of concern for social norms hinders his progress. He hopes to be reunited with a human from his past.
"Stay back. I don't know your scent."

## Appearance
Lyca has black hair with blonde ends. His hair is longer in the back and shorter in the front. He wears a golden helix piercing on his right ear and has golden yellow eyes.

When his arms are exposed, he has multiple scars from bite marks on them. Something he is shown to do to himself when in his half-wolf form in order to stop himself from biting others.[1]

While he was in the prison cell, his hair was longer and messier until he became a student.

### Partial wolf
In his partial wolf form, Lyca looks like his fully human form except for his wolf ears and a tail that matches his hair.

### Half-wolf
In his half-wolf form, Lyca has grey fur covering his body. His palms, nose, mouth, and the underside of his tail are a lighter shade of grey. He also has visible ears and tails, a snout, claws, and sharp teeth.

## Personality
Lyca is easily wary of new people, and it can take him a while to get used to being around them. He gets irritated when he hears rumors about him or anyone making fun of him, and isn't afraid of calling them out and stand his ground. Lyca is determined and willing to learn new things.

However, there are times where he chooses to ignore basic etiquette for his own comfort and satisfaction (e.g eating Sho's chicken over rice with his bare hands or shoving his face in the food container).

Lyca can be short-tempered, especially when he is being nagged or reminded of things to do by Rui. Despite being unfamiliar of his surroundings in Darkwick Academy, he's quick to adjust to how things work there. Lyca can be compliant and obedient when he needs to be, although he can be stubborn at times.

Despite Lyca's harsh words and mannerisms, he is a good person at heart. During his first day attending classes, he thanked a Hotarubi student for lending him a pencil during class. He also stopped himself from harming the MC when he was transforming into a werewolf on the full moon.

However in Episode 10, after the Karuizawa Resort butler he had bonded with mistook him and the other two Obscuary ghouls as having murdered Ms. Takamine, Mr. Goto calls him a "monster" while proceeding to use the same ice pick Lyca gives him earlier as self-defense, against Lyca himself. This, plus the accusatory reactions of the rest of the resort staff made him fall into a relapse for quite some time, to the point he refused to leave his room after the group returned to Darkwick.

Lyca is also somewhat protective of the MC, whether it be from Edward and Rui's flirtatious advances or from danger. He reacts negatively to the former, often telling Edward and Rui to get away from the MC or not to touch her.

Author note: Lyca is very mindful and protective toward the MC and Subaru. Jo passed his "genuinely good person" test.

## Background
In Episode 5, when Lyca was first introduced to MC, Haku, and Zenji by Subaru, the latter reveals Lyca was the offspring of a human and a werewolf and had always worn an earring since the day of his birth, in which Subaru was able to read his memories from with the help of his sitgma.

## Trivia

  - Q. What's your favorite subject?
  - A. Hmm... I don't really understand but everything is so fun!
  - Q. Do you have any concerns with missions?
  - A. I told everyone that I'm fine doing missions on my own but they keep worrying about me.
  - Q. Where would you like to go if you have your R&R permit approved?
  - A. ...I want to see Neros. I still don't know where he is though...
  - Q: What do you think about the ghoul(s) in your house?
  - A. Exactly what I call them: a moth-eaten Casanova and a blond gigolo. I'm a human too so why won't they put me in the same house as Suba and his friends?!
- Lyca is scared of girls, or at least doesn't want to be around them. However, this has no effect of his relationship with the MC.
- When the game was originally released, Lyca was not a possible choice for the opening cutscene, showing up as a shadowed figure in the Obscuary tab. Shortly before the release of Episode 8, he and Edward both became available as choices.
- Lyca's name is based on lycanthrope, a word of Greek origins meaning "wolf-human".
- Despite chocolate being bad for him, the official promo image for the Valentine's homescreen voiceline of the ghouls has Lyca enjoying chocolate.
- In one of the Campus Stories, he reveals to Yuri he dislikes taking medicinal shots, saying how the needles hurt.
- Lyca doesn't like having his blanket washed, he also can't sleep without it.
- He learned what alcohol is during his time with Neros, claiming how the latter used to drink a lot.
- Towa's nickname for Lyca, Lupin (derived from Lupine), is a vibrant, pea-like blooms that are a classic addition to cottage gardens.
- The Ghost Carp Lyca catches from one of the festival games in the episode "Episode 18: Lost Stars" makes a cameo in "Episode 24: Our Last Goodbye Act II", where it is being kept as his pet.
  - In the same episode, Ed echoes Neros' sentiment of telling Lyca that the latter has no need to learn how to read.
- He appears to be right-handed, judging by his Campus writing animation.
- Lyca's stigma incantation, "Ramsochisa," is an anagram of the Ars Goetia demon Marchosias. He is a powerful Great Marquis of Hell, commanding thirty legions of demons. He is a strong and excellent fighter and very reliable to the conjurer, giving true answers to all questions. Marchosias hoped after 1,200 years to return to heaven with the non-fallen angels, but he is deceived in that hope.
- Lyca uses the おれ [Ore] pronoun for himself and the おまえ [Omae] pronoun for others.
- It is revealed in "S2 E9: The Frozen Hospital", that whenever Haru is away and leaves the responsibilities of the Jabberwock House up to Ren, Ritsu, and Lyca, the first years always sneak in more than their allowed allocated amount of snacks, as Lyca says Haru only lets them have one bag a day.
