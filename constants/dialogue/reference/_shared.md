## Ghouls, stigmas, and artifacts
Source: "Tokyo Debunker Stigmas, Artifacts, And Other Possibly Combat Relevant Information" compiled by danieyells (tumblr, post 757490708898889728; updated 8/24/2026, Frostheim through S02E07). Contains story spoilers: plot reveals and sensitive notes here are spark tier and up only, never new/known/warm. Lines tagged [spark+] are the flagged ones. "?" and question marks are the compiler's: unconfirmed or not yet revealed.

Ghouls are humanoids with enhanced strength and special powers due to having a wish granted by a demon. Thus far a demon is not an entity that is seen or encountered, nor is becoming a ghoul a deliberately made choice--when a person makes a wish while in distress, a demon may choose to grant it. The person who makes the wish will only hear "Shall I grant your wish?" in response, and find that it's been granted in a somewhat twisted way soon after, and the incantation for their stigma is placed into their head sometime after. Normally someone who makes a wish dies a horrible death some time after or becomes a soulless husk, but those who survive without this become ghouls. This is very rare, on top of the rarity of wishes being granted. With the exception of Jo(bigender, birth sex unknown) all ghouls shown in the game have been male. (In the original concept for Tokyo Debunker only males could become ghouls. It's unknown if this remains true in the release version.) Aside from humans, a merfolk(Shion), a vampire(Edward), a half-werewolf(Lyca), and whatever Towa is have also become ghouls, but all of them so far seem to have at least partially human forms, and in Ed's case it's unknown if he became a vampire or ghoul first.
A stigma is the power granted to a ghoul by their pact with a demon. The incantation one recites to activate their stigma is, at some point not too long after their wish is granted, implanted in their mind as if simply placed there. Incantations typically must be spoken aloud to activate them, however skilled ghouls can recite their incantations in their heads, some stigmas are passive, and some sigmas activate of their own accord. The stigma's ability to function, intensity of its kickback, and the ghoul's overall control of it seems to be connected to their mental state.  Thus far, a stigma has not been depicted failing while MC was strengthening it with the exception of in Episode 2, which may have been Sho pretending rather than an actual failure of MC's ring's ability to activate. Each incantation is an anagram(scrambling of letters) of the name of the demon they made a pact with. The demon in question often relates in some way to the wish made, stigma granted, or ghoul themself. All ghouls only have one stigma, but some stigmas have a rather broad reach of power.
Special artifacts are anomalous items loaned to a Darkwick Academy student by the school. Special artifacts choose their users--they won't work for anyone but the person they've chosen. Due to the legal paperwork involved, a student's first Anomalous Law class of the year is when they enter the artifact storeroom, a room at the back of Darkwick's training area where practical Anomalous Combat classes are typically held. Everyone is allowed only one special artifact at a time. Every year, at the beginning of the year, the opportunity to change special artifacts is presented to the returning students. Common artifacts--a type of anomalous object of which there are many, which have seemingly better understood and simpler uses--can be loaned for missions and students with favor can borrow them longterm.

## Non-roster characters
Same source. Faculty and other ghouls who aren't playable here.

### Enzo Natori
- Gives brutal punishments, which Rui says could be even worse than the Institute's
- Has a habit of taking punishments into his own hands, including his own.

### Shiki
- Specializes in western magic
- Still in contact with Rui, perhaps over VR chat
- Has a history of holing up underground and never showing his face
- May be quite delicate, with Elias saying that the death game during Deadly In Love would have made him faint

### Yamato
- (Source labels these notes: "analysis, unconfirmed information".)
- Currently forced into containment in the form of a white fox.
- Intelligence is supposed to have been limited, to the point of being unable to open the gate containing him, but he seems to get out of containment whenever Hyde leaves. It's unclear how much of his awareness remains.
- Seems to violently dislike Haru.
- Most likely the person Haku is looking for, as he reacted with recognition when he saw him, and Haku was looking for someone kept in containment.
- Like Haku he seems to specialize in spirits.
- May have attempted to escape Darkwick?

### Hyde
- One of his eyes seems to grant him the ability to see things that wouldn't ordinarily be visible, although it's not clear in what way.
- Overusing his eye makes him tired.
- World's leading anomalous biologist.
- Former employee of the Victor Institute For Artificial Anomaly Research, which shows in that he has artificial anomalous effect bombs that work specifically with Romeo's stigma made for him to help him capture anomalies.
- Says that he's part of why Hodge, Podge, and Moby are in their current forms. However, with enough money he can get new, suitable vessels for Hodge and Podge if need be. Seems to imply that he can places souls into new bodies if need be?

### Moby
- His tentacles are apparently incredibly strong, and he's able to stop a large amount of anomalies at once(although he says too much is being asked of him to do so.

### Nicolas
- Not much of a fighter
- Created the anomalous medicine that near instantly heals wounds
