Yuri 
The domineering captain of Mortkranken, which doubles as a medical facility and houses students who specialize in anomalous medicine. Sly and calculating, he will take any opportunity to turn anomalies and other ghouls into lab rats for his experiments.
“
Allow me to grant you the privilege of becoming my test subject.
”
Appearance
Yuri has short, teal colored hair, teal eyes, pale white skin, a double lobe piercing on his right ear and a single lobe piercing on his left.

He is usually seen wearing his school uniform, which consists of a white button up shirt beneath a black buttoned up blazer, black pants, a black and teal necktie and white gloves.

He is also seen wearing his lab uniform which consists of a grey button up shirt, black pants, a black necktie with a teal stripe down the center, black gloves, a black leather chest harness, a black choker and an unbuttoned white lab coat.

Personality
Yuri is an arrogant, snobby, and ambitious man with a lot of confidence in his abilities, calling himself the "genius and visionary Dr. Yuri Isami".

He is hardworking and determined, as he has a goal of being the pioneer of anomalous medical science.

His desire to be acknowledged by the world is his main motivator in making progress in his research, so he takes his duties as a physician seriously and puts his all into everything he does.

Yuri is diligent in his studies and expresses pride in his accomplishments, having a habit of showing off while taking pleasure in being recognized and praised. He is also fiercely competitive and tends to be quite aggressive with those he considers his rivals. He often flaunts his knowledge while looking down on others he deems less intelligent than himself.

In contrast to his stoic assistant, Jiro, Yuri appears to be a very expressive and easily flustered person. He is short-tempered and has difficulty controlling his emotions.

He has some quirks, like humming classical music and having a poor sense of direction. As apparent from his Affinity 23 and 24 dialogues, Yuri struggles with the idea of losing patients.

In Episode 14: The Infectious Death Wish when two of Frostheim students humiliated Yuri during a medical check-up, Yuri raises his voice at them, but shortly after is speechless. When the two students leave Yuri starts crying, revealing that he might be a much more sensitive and insecure person than he appears.

Tohma
Tohma Ishibashi (磴 塔真 Ishibashi Tōma) is a third year student at Darkwick Academy and the vice-captain of Frostheim House.

He is not afraid to get his hands dirty in pursuit of his goals and has been running things in Jin's absence. His monocle masks the insidious glint in his eye.
"I lend a sympathetic ear to those in need—that's all."

Appearance
Tohma has ashy-blonde hair, pale skin, pale blue eyes, sharp features, and often wears a monocle on his left eye. Jin describes him as having a lot of brute strength, Yuri calls him "musclehead", and he is shown to be almost as tall as Alan (one of the tallest ghouls).[1]

Personality
Tohma is known to have a cunning gaze. Contrary to his appearance, Tohma is a rather easygoing and responsible person. He runs Frostheim for Jin even when it's a tiring job, or whenever he's on a mission.

He is also open and enthusiastic if the MC needs some or help with work.

Background
Tohma used to be a Vagastrom student in until the middle of last year[2], with his transfer being first implied by Yumiko before she gets cut off by him in the first episode.

In the first episode, Yumiko Sumitomo implies that Tohma may have orchestrated the downfall of one of Jin Kamurai's close friends, filling in the void "almost as though it was planned", and possibly played a role in the transfer of aforementioned friend to another house. This, however, remains speculation.

Towa
Towa Otonashi (音無 叶空 Otonashi Towa) is a second year student at Darkwick Academy and the vice-captain of the Jabberwock House who loves flowers and playing with bubbles.

Towa has a deep fondness for flowers. Though strikingly whimsical and capricious, he is quite attached to Haru. He has a peculiar skill for making supernatural creatures do his bidding.

Appearance
Towa has short messy white-lavender hair. His eyes are peach-colored with horizontal pupils akin to that of a goat's, as well as distinct lower lashes.

In Towa’s Darkwick uniform, he wears a loose beige sweater over a collared and buttoned white shirt. His tie is the orange-black patterned one for Jabberwock. He has black pants.

During the flashback in Episode 22, like Haru, he had slightly longer and messier hair, with random pieces of flowers and petals scattered on it.

Personality
From his first appearance he appears as a strange and eccentric individual, with a great love for flowers and animals. Towa is always happy and seems to love everything around him (with various exceptions).

He also has animalistic behaviors: like eating flowers and drinking water directly from the tap, Towa actually seems more like an animal than a person.

In Episode 3: It Came From The Sea, he immediately appears friendly with the MC and, as described by herself, has a "pleasant" presence. Towa seems to have a very developed sense of hearing and also seems capable of reading people well, understanding who is kind-hearted and who is more hostile.

But, in reality, it is hinted that Towa could have a much darker personality. This aspect of him is shown on several occasions: very often he stops smiling and his eyes become thinner, but not only that, his voice also becomes deeper, the sky becomes cloudier and lightning and rain fall. An example would be when, in the princess castle at the bottom of the sea, Towa gets colder when a turtle tries to stop him from leaving the room. Another example is when Ren treats Haru badly or when he talks to Edward.

Edward observes with amazement that he didn't expect Towa become attached "to such small weak creatures". In Episode 10: Tea With A Twist Of Murder, Edward comments to Towa that his real face is more "charming".
