# Darkwick bot: domain glossary

## Missions

- **Culprit**: the student a riddle mission is about; the riddle's correct answer.
- **Accusation**: a wrong riddle guess that names a real character. That character is **the accused**.
- **Miss**: a wrong riddle guess that names nobody.
- **Culprit reveal**: what a player sees on solving a riddle: the culprit caught out, their riddle winning line, and the reward.
- **Riddle winning line**: the culprit's authored line in a culprit reveal; one per riddle, the same at every relationship tier.
- **Debrief**: the private follow-up each player in a completed co-op opens: one student from that co-op's house, drawn for that player alone, and the reward. One per player per co-op.
- **Debrief line**: the drawn student's authored line in a debrief; the same at every relationship tier.
